Common Questions

Are my purchased leads exclusive? ▼
Yes. Each lead is sold once through our storefront. After purchase, it is removed from available inventory and saved to your account. Other buyers can purchase different leads in the same county.
How often does the data refresh? ▼
Most counties refresh weekly — we re-pull public notice filings every 7 days. Florida counties (Duval, Clay, Nassau, St. Johns) refresh daily from the Jax Daily Record. Michigan counties update weekly as new filings are posted.
What does a skip-traced lead include? ▼
Phone number and email sourced from public records and people-search databases. Hit rate is roughly 65% — so plan on about 65 out of every 100 leads coming back with contact data. Contact availability varies by lead.
What counties are available right now? ▼
We currently cover {{ county_count }} counties across multiple states. Browse the storefront and filter by county to see live inventory.
Is there a setup fee or contract? ▼
No setup fee or monthly subscription. Pay only for the individual leads or prepaid credit packs you choose. Full lead details unlock after purchase.
